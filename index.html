Classic vs modern encryption

Classic ciphers (Caesar, Vigenere, Playfair) use small key spaces and keep letter patterns visible. They fall to brute force and frequency analysis, quickly with a computer.

Modern encryption (AES in WPA2/WPA3, TLS in HTTPS) uses public, heavily tested algorithms with large keys. Security comes from the key, not a secret method (Kerckhoffs's principle). WPA2 derives its key from your password with PBKDF2 hashing, and WPA3 uses SAE, a password-based key exchange. Either way, a weak or widely shared password weakens protection.
